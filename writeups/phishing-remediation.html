Once confirmed as a true positive, the following containment and remediation actions were taken:

Step 1 — Email removal

Deleted the malicious email from every affected mailbox across the organization to prevent further engagement.

Step 2 — Domain/URL blocking

Step 3 — Credential reset

Reset passwords for any users identified as having interacted with the malicious link, out of precaution.

Step 4 — Monitoring

Monitored affected accounts for a period following remediation to confirm no further suspicious activity occurred.
