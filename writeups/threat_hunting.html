Proactive Threat Hunting: MITRE ATT&CK-Mapped Investigation & Remediation

Role: Threat Hunter Severity: High Outcome: Threat Hunting & Incident Response
Splunk SIEM Microsoft Advance Hunting Suspicious Application/Program Incident Response

Overview

Conducted a proactive threat hunt targeting tactics, techniques, and procedures (TTPs) associated with threat actor MuddyWater, known to target organizations through various attacks like malware deployment, DLL side-loading, and command-and-control communications to maintain persistence and exfiltrate senstive data. This hypothesis-driven hunt were based on behavior patterns mapped to the MITRE ATT&CK framework, rather than triggered by an existing alert.

This writeup covers the full threat hunt process from investigation, incident response, escalation actions, and outcome.

Hypothesis

Based on MuddyWater's documented use of spearphishing links, remote administration tools (e.g Anydesk) and authorized software for command-and-control (C2) communication, this hunt focuses on identifying unusual or unauthorized use of applications on employee endpoints that can be used to establish outbound connections enabling attackers to take advantage and exploit.

Investigation

Steps taken to confirm this was a true positive rather than a false alarm:

  • Implemented KQL queries in Microsoft Advance Hunting (DeviceFileEvents) to look for endpoint device logs running applications not part of the organization's approved software baseline.
    Result: Identified an unauthorized application (Openclaw) running on 3 endpoint devices.
  • Implemented KQL queries in Microsoft Advance Hunting (DeviceNetworkEvents) to investigate the connection further.
    Result: Confirmed the application (Openclaw) was actively downloaded by the employee from a github resource.
  • Reviewed the device's timeline in Microsoft Defender, which showed the application making outbound connections to unknown IP addresses.
    Result: Confirmed active communication with external destinations (IPs/domains) outside business activity, which were tagged with a malicious confidence rating of 69+/100 by Microsoft Defender Threat Intelligence.

Tools:
Splunk SIEM Microsoft Defender Advance Hunting Google Threat Intelligence Domain & IP Validators (Virus Total, Talos, Whois)

Remediation

After confirming that the unauthorized application has active outbound communication with malicious IPs the following containment and remediation actions were taken:

Step 1 — Disable Application

Disabled the unauthorized application (Openclaw) via Microsoft Defender to immediately halt its outbound connections to the malicious infrastructure.

Step 2 — Isolated Device

The device was isolated from the network to ensure the no malicious content or activities were occuring within the endpoint that could cause harm to the organization network.

Step 3 — Ticket Creation & Escalation

Created a ServiceNow ticket with the relevant information and escalated it to the appropriate service team for remediation.

Step 4 — Application Removal

Coordinated removal of the application from the affected endpoint and requested a full antivirus scan to confirm no further compromise.

Step 5 — Monitoring

Monitored the device post-remediation to confirm no further outbound connections to any malicious IP addresses occurred.

Step 6 — Documentation

Compiled the full findings and remediation actions from the threat hunt into a structured report, shared with senior leadership to provide visibility into the investigation process and outcome.

Outcome

The unauthorized application was successfully removed from the affected endpoint, eliminating the identified command-and-control communication channel. No further connections to the malicious infrastructure were observed following remediation.

Lessons learned

This hunt reinforced the value of hypothesis-driven threat hunting mapped to known adversary TTPs, rather than waiting for an alert, actively searching for behavior patterns tied to a specific threat actor surfaced a threat that automated detection had not yet flagged.
